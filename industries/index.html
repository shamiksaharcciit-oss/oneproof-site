Examples by industry

The same record, on pipelines like yours.

Each example is a small pipeline in the live demo, with a change planted in some of its runs. Each page shows the steps, what was changed, and what onetrace shows, from the demo's own pages. The data is invented, recorded when the demo was built, and the demo controls nothing.

Examples

Industrial maintenance

Vibration readings to a work order. A calibration offset located at its first difference, a firmware change that moves no output, a declined slot.
